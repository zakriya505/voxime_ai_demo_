Frequently Asked Questions

Before you put Voxime.ai on the line.

1. How does Voxime.ai work?+

When a customer calls your business number, our AI receptionist answers instantly. It greets them professionally, answers their questions, collects their information, and books the appointment directly into your calendar — all automatically, in real time.

2. Do I need to be tech-savvy to use Voxime.ai?+

Not at all. We handle the entire setup for you. Once your system is configured, everything runs automatically. You just receive your appointment confirmations.

3. What happens if a customer asks a question the AI doesn't know?+

Your AI is trained specifically for your business. It knows your services, your availability, and how to respond to common questions. For anything outside its scope, it professionally collects the customer's information for a follow-up.

4. Will my customers know they're talking to an AI?+

Our AI speaks naturally and professionally, delivering fast and friendly service. Voxime.ai fully complies with all applicable state regulations — your business is always protected.

5. What if I want to change my script or services?+

On the Pro plan, you get one script adjustment per month. Changes are handled by our team quickly and efficiently.

6. Is there a long-term contract?+

No. Voxime.ai operates month-to-month. You can cancel anytime with no penalties.

7. How quickly can I get started?+

Once you sign up, your system is configured and ready within 48 business hours.

8. What languages does the AI speak?+

Currently English. Spanish support is coming soon.

9. How do I receive appointment confirmations?+

You receive an email notification for every appointment booked. Pro plan clients also receive SMS confirmations sent directly to their customers.

10. What if I already have a receptionist?+

Voxime.ai works alongside your existing team, handling after-hours calls, overflow calls, and peak periods so nothing ever falls through the cracks.
